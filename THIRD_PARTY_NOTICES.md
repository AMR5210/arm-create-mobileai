# Third-party notices

External models, datasets, libraries, and methods this project depends on.

Every license below was read from its primary source — the package's own license
file, the Hugging Face card metadata, or SPDX headers in the vendored source — not
inferred from documentation. Where a claim could not be established that way, it is
marked as such rather than guessed.

## Two different licenses cover two different things

This repository publishes two distinct artifacts under two distinct licenses. The
distinction is not cosmetic:

| | Covered by | License | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The code in this repository** | training scripts, `Q2_K` encoder, benchmark harness, iOS app, analysis tooling | **MIT** | [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) |
| **The published model artifact** | the QAT `Q2_K` GGUF on Hugging Face, a derivative of Qwen3-0.6B | **Apache-2.0** | [model card](https://huggingface.co/AMR5210/qwen3-0.6b-qat-q2k) |

The model weights are **not** MIT-licensed. They are a fine-tune of Qwen3-0.6B and
inherit that model's Apache-2.0 terms. Cloning this repository under MIT grants no
rights over the weights; downloading the weights grants no rights over the code.

**A constraint on the weights that Apache-2.0 alone does not express:** the training
blend includes Alpaca, which is CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial). See
[Training data](#training-data) below — this is flagged rather than resolved here.

## Training data

| Dataset | Source | License | Verified from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alpaca | [`tatsu-lab/alpaca`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tatsu-lab/alpaca) | **CC BY-NC 4.0** | card metadata + card text |
| WikiText-2 (train split) | [`Salesforce/wikitext`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Salesforce/wikitext) | **CC BY-SA 3.0** and **GFDL** | card metadata |
| FineWeb | [`HuggingFaceFW/fineweb`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb) | **ODC-By 1.0** | card metadata |

Loaded by [`qat/data.py`](qat/data.py). The featured checkpoint used 44,000 Alpaca
examples, 4,889 WikiText-2 train examples, and 43,705 FineWeb examples.

**Alpaca is non-commercial, and this warrants review before any commercial use.**
Its card states the dataset "is available under the Creative Commons NonCommercial
(CC BY-NC 4.0)". It was also "generated by OpenAI's `text-davinci-003` engine", so
OpenAI's terms at time of generation may impose further restrictions on derivatives.

Stanford's own Alpaca release took the position that the restriction does reach
weights: its usage notice states the dataset allows only non-commercial use, and that
models trained on the dataset should not be used outside research purposes. Part of
their reasoning does not transfer — their base model was LLaMA, itself under a
non-commercial license, whereas this project's base is Qwen3-0.6B under Apache-2.0.
Their reading of the *data* restriction stands independently of that.

The published GGUF is a model trained partly on this data while being distributed
under Apache-2.0, inherited from Qwen3-0.6B. Whether — and how — CC BY-NC propagates
to model weights trained on the data is genuinely unsettled, and is a legal question
this file does not attempt to answer. Anyone considering commercial use should
evaluate it independently or consult counsel. It is recorded here so nobody relies on
the Apache-2.0 label without knowing.

WikiText-2's dual CC BY-SA 3.0 / GFDL terms reflect its Wikipedia origin. Note that
the training blend deliberately includes the WikiText-2 **train** split, which is
why the WikiText-2 perplexity result is domain-aligned; see the limitations in
[`README.md`](README.md). The **test** split was never trained on.

## Methods adapted from published research

Full citations, with arXiv identifiers, are in the
[References section of `README.md`](README.md#references) — not duplicated here. What
this project took from each:

| Work | What was used here |
|---|---|
| Apple, *Apple Intelligence Foundation Language Models: Tech Report 2025* | Precedent and motivation for 2-bit weight quantization as a viable on-device target |
| **BitDistiller** | The **CAKLD distillation objective**, used directly as this project's training loss (`--distill-loss cakld`) |
| **ParetoQ** | The **SEQ quantizer**, reimplemented and evaluated as an alternative to affine fake-quant; measured as a tie and not adopted |
| **LLM-QAT** | General QAT-with-distillation framing for LLMs |
| **EfficientQAT** | Staged-optimization QAT framing, as background for the training schedule |

No code was copied from these works; the implementations in
[`qat/`](qat/) are this project's own. `Q2_K` block-format encoding in
[`qat/gguf_q2k.py`](qat/gguf_q2k.py) is a from-scratch NumPy port of ggml's
`quantize_row_q2_K_ref` / `make_qkx2_quants` reference behaviour, and is therefore
derived from llama.cpp's MIT-licensed algorithm rather than from any paper.
